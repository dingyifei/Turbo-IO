The wide cover card for the project: the wordmark on the left and, on the right, the lens slab with two phone satellites, drawn from the system's own colours and priority bands.

**Consumer provides:** nothing. It is a fixed 960×288 picture (the card height is 288) with no state.

**Rules**
- The motif is the priority bands: three strips inside the lens slab rising in brightness and height toward the bottom (`lens-mid`, `lens-high`, `lens-full`), plus the 24×3 focus caret. Priority runs bottom-up.
- The phone satellites use `accent-fill` and `accent-soft`; the lens slab uses `lens-black`. Slab sides are `space-4` multiples and the gutters are `space-4`. Corners are `radius-md` on slabs and `radius-xs` on strips and the caret.
- The wordmark "Turbo IO" is set at 88px, bottom left, in the display font.
- The measurements and the reasoning are in the comment inside [preview.html](preview.html); change that and this file together.
