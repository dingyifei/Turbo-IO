Wake screen: what we know, and what a redesign needs
It is the stock launcher's BoardView (BoardView.cpp in nuttx_ap.bin):
• SysinfoWidget: date, weather, battery, connection, the LifeLog icon
• Card stack with page dots: Weather, To-do, Schedule, Stock, Mail, World clock, and A2UI cards (ours goes here)
• Minimal mode: a large clock plus battery
• A lottie overlay when the knob wakes the glasses
What we control today: only the inside of our 256 × 194 card slot (the dc_text hook). The clock, date, battery, weather, dots and the layout are all stock.
To redesign the rest:
1. A photo of the current wake screen through the lens. There is no screenshot or render anywhere, and positions, fonts and intensities are unknown.
2. Static RE of the 1.0.5.5 addresses for BoardView, SysinfoWidget and MinimalClockWidget (they're named in the 1.0.4.12 symbols). Read-only, no device.
3. New patch sites, at least one per redrawn widget. That's beyond TMG8's "no new sites" rule, so it would be its own line (TMG9), with its own audit and OTA cross-check.
The sketch on the right is a target to aim the RE at, not a spec. It follows the design system: the time is the primary item, at the bottom in lens-hero; date and weather are ambient, in band T; the card slot keeps its stock size, so the A2UI cards (ours and RayNeo's) still fit.
