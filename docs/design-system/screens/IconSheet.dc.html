Each icon is a 48 × 48 SVG with a 2.6-unit round stroke and no fills. The SVGs are the source of truth; TMG8 commits them with an encoder that rebuilds focus_menu_icons.h byte for byte. 1° peak = the mean of level2.2 over the brightest 25 × 25 px window, the same metric as the Lens Brightness Audit. Limits: 0.45 for a focused icon, 0.20 for an unfocused one. Unfocused icons keep the TMG7 gain (0x77); they read dimmer because the strokes are thin. If they're too faint on the glasses, the fix is a 3.0 stroke, not a higher gain.
